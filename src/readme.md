# ENCY Pulse

**Know the moment ENCY is done, wherever you are.**

Long toolpath calculations and simulations do not need you sitting in front of the screen. ENCY Pulse watches them for you and sends a push to your phone, a tap on your Apple Watch, or an email the moment they finish. Start the job, walk away, and come back exactly when it is ready.

## Two ways to be notified

**Entire project** — one message when the whole job is done.

- “Project calculation completed.”
- “Project simulation completed.”

**Selected operations** — tick the operations you care about; each one reports its own completion, by name, the second it finishes.

- “Operation 'Roughing 01' calculation completed.”
- “Simulation completed for 'Finishing 02'.”

Pick them in the ENCY Pulse window (Utilities → ENCY Pulse) or right-click any operation in ENCY's tree. Failed operations are reported too, so you never come back to a surprise.

## Delivery, your way

| Channel | What you need |
|---|---|
| **Phone push (ntfy)** | The free ntfy app and your personal code, generated for you. Apple Watch mirrors the iPhone alert. |
| **Phone and Apple Watch (Pushover)** | A Pushover account; native watch app with message history. |
| **Email** | Your SMTP login. |
| **Company relay** | Address and key from your administrator. |

Press **Send test message** and watch it arrive within seconds.

## Stays out of your way

- **Only when I'm away**: nothing is sent while you are typing or moving the mouse. You can see the progress bar yourself.
- **Quiet hours** hold phone pushes until the morning.
- **Ignore short runs** keeps two-second recalculations silent.

## Made for ENCY

- Looks like ENCY: follows the active light or dark theme, or pick one manually.
- Runs quietly in the background and never blocks ENCY while it works.
- Settings and rules are stored for your Windows account only; passwords and tokens are encrypted. Only the message text ever leaves the computer.
